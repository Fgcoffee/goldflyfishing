3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To provide the service you ask for — your account, saved decks, history and sign-in (performance of a contract, UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the service secure and working — rate limits, preventing abuse, investigating faults (our legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To check simulation results and improve the engine — keeping the runs played in your browser, replaying a sample to confirm their results, and finding cards the engine plays wrongly (our legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To send you service emails — address verification and password resets, only when you ask for them (performance of a contract). We send no marketing.
  • To meet legal obligations where they apply (Art. 6(1)(c)).

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or profile you.

5. Who else is involved

  • Render Services, Inc. hosts the site and its database, in its Frankfurt (EU) region. Render is a US company; transfers are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum / standard contractual clauses in its data processing terms.
  • Discord, if you choose to sign in with Discord. Discord’s own privacy policy applies to your Discord account.
  • Scryfall — card images and mana symbols load directly from Scryfall’s servers, so Scryfall receives your IP address and browser details when your browser fetches them. Discord avatars load from Discord’s servers in the same way.
  • EDHREC and Scryfall’s API are queried by our server for card recommendations; no personal data is sent to them.
  • Archidekt, only if you use the Archidekt import.
  • An email provider, once email sign-in is enabled, to deliver verification and reset emails.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything saved to it: until you delete your account.
  • Unverified email accounts: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Sign-in sessions: 30 days (or until you log out). Email links: 48 hours (verification) or 30 minutes (password reset), and they work once.
  • Anonymous simulation sessions: in memory only, gone after a few hours of inactivity or a server restart.
  • Simulations run in your browser: in your account until you delete the account; if you were not signed in, 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Our host’s request logs: according to Render’s log retention (days, not months).

7. How we protect it

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with argon2id. Session IDs and email-link tokens are stored only as hashes, so a copy of the database cannot be used to sign in. Access to your saved data is limited to your own account.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to our use of it. Most of this you can do yourself under Log in → your account:

  • Download my data gives you everything we hold about your account, as JSON.
  • Delete account permanently removes your account, decks, history, verdicts and sessions.
  • Your display name can be changed there; for anything else, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

9. Children

Goldfisher is for people aged 16 and over. Do not create an account if you are younger.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so on the site and, where needed, ask you to agree again. The version above changes with every revision.
