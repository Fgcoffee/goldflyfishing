# Spot check, 2026-10-26: 500 random cards checked by hand

Sheet: `docs/spot-check/2026-10-26.md` (every card, its text, the parser's
reading and the verdict). Verdicts alone: `2026-10-26.verdicts`. Tool:
`python -m mtgfish.tools.spot_check` (`sample`, `summarize`).

## Method

500 cards drawn uniformly at random, with the operating system's random
source, from the 32,765 cards legal in at least one format. Each card's oracle
text was compared with the parser's reading of every ability. Where the
rendered reading hid a detail (target counts, durations, whose hand), the IR
itself was checked, and where the IR could not settle it the card was played
on a board.

## Result

The question asked of each card is whether the parser **read** it correctly:
does the IR say what the card says. Where the reading is right but the rules
engine, or a keyword's expansion in `mtgfish/rules`, plays it wrong, the card
is marked ENGINE - logged, and not counted against the reading.

| Verdict | Cards | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| OK - fully read, and read right | 219 | 43.8% |
| PARTIAL - some abilities unread (honestly), the read ones right | 128 | 25.6% |
| UNREAD - nothing usable read, and the card says so | 114 | 22.8% |
| **WRONG - misread: counted as understood, but the IR says something else** | **23** | **4.6%** |
| ENGINE - read right, played wrong by the engine | 12 | 2.4% |
| UNSURE - the reading could not be settled | 4 | 0.8% |

**Of the 250 cards the census counts as fully read, 19 are misread (7.6%).**
Nine more of them are read right and played wrong by the engine.

Seven PARTIAL cards are stronger than the real card because the part left
unread was a drawback or a restriction: Savage Beating (castable outside
combat), Changeling Titan (champion inert), Benevolent Geist (no exile, so
disturb repeats), Faerie Impostor and Mox Diamond (no cost on entering),
Vintara Elephant (keeps trample), Vivid Marsh (enters untapped). That is the
"weaker only" rule the skeleton parse is meant to enforce.

## Misread (23 cards), by cause

**A target became "all"**: Phase Dolphin, Chainwhip Cyclops, Insufferable
Balladeer - "target creature can't block / be blocked" reads as every
creature.

**The wrong player or zone**: Architects of Will (the target player looks and
orders, not you), Witness the End (you lose the life), Borrowed Knowledge and
Viseling (count every hand), Soul-Guide Lantern (exiles your graveyard too).

**"or" read as "and", and other list grammar**: Skullport Merchant ("another
creature or a Treasure" became "a Treasure creature"), Satsuki ("Saga or
enchantment creature"), Relentless Pursuit ("a creature card and/or a land
card"), Llanowar Wastes (the pain applies only to {G}, not {B} - the whole
pain-land cycle), Chandra, Spark Hunter (the reward became a third mode),
Jaya Ballard ("up to three" became one).

**Keyword arguments lost**: "Affinity for snow lands" / "for enchantments"
(Icebreaker Kraken, Brine Giant) - the "for ..." is not read, so the
quality is empty and the builder falls back to artifacts. "Equip Hero {3}" and
"Equip planeswalker {1}" (Hulkbuster Armor, Luxior) - the cost and the
restriction are lost: free, onto any creature.

**Type changes**: Enduring Innocence ("It's an enchantment" adds the type
instead of setting it, so it returns as a creature and recurs), Omnibian
(adds Frog instead of replacing the creature types).

**Lost scaling or targeting**: Fists of Flame (the "for each card drawn"
amount is put on a wrapper, not on the +1/+0), Arcade Cabinet ("up to four
target creatures" not marked targeted).

**Known**: Planar Collapse - "They can't be regenerated" is the auditor's F32.

## Read right, played wrong by the engine (12 cards)

Each of these is a keyword or keyword action whose word and number or cost
were read correctly; its expansion in `mtgfish/rules` is wrong:

- Echo (Stingscourger, Firemaw Kavu) and cumulative upkeep (Flow of Maggots,
  Kjeldoran Javelineer, Mystic Might): the upkeep payment never sacrifices
  the permanent (checked on a board); echo's "since your last upkeep" is
  also just "you control it".
- Toxic (Crawling Chorus, Duelist of Deep Faith): poisons every opponent.
- Outlast (Salt Road Patrol): no {T} in the cost.
- Basic landcycling (Sylvan Reclamation): searches for every basic land.
- Bolster (Anafenza): a counter on every creature.
- Incubate (Sculpted Perfection): no counters, no transform.
- Detain (New Prahv Guildmage): never ends.

## Unsure

Masterwork of Ingenuity (one chosen Equipment?), Resonating Lute
(both mana the same color?), Nightveil Specter and Fallen Shinobi (whose
library is exiled from?).

## What stops the parser most often

From the census of the whole pool (17,263 of 32,402 cards fully read). The
word a failed ability stopped on, by abilities:

| Stopped at | Abilities | | Stopped at (two words) | Abilities |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| if | 1,556 | | you may | 509 |
| you | 1,038 | | as long | 340 |
| whenever | 984 | | whenever you | 331 |
| this | 722 | | this creature | 263 |
| target | 586 | | if a | 260 |
| that | 510 | | whenever a | 244 |
| choose | 477 | | if you | 213 |
| when | 447 | | for each | 208 |
| each | 427 | | when you | 206 |
| as | 406 | | if this | 206 |

These are mostly where a second sentence or clause begins: the first
sentence read, and the follow-up ("If you do", "You may", "As long as",
"For each") did not attach to it.

Words never read anywhere in the pool, by cards: still (149), otherwise (143),
remains (129), died (119), chooses (105), emblem (94), while (93), alone (84),
steps (81), been (73), cycle (73), half (72), rounded (68), paid (66).

Largest clusters by construct: inert abilities (1,266), costs the cost reader
cannot read (323), activation restrictions (173), "you become the monarch"
(38), "until this leaves the battlefield" (33), the werewolf day/night
triggers (63), "can't be blocked by creatures with power 2 or less" (32).
